NOVELTY

Most of z/halve's privacy is borrowed. This page separates what this repository puts together from the pieces it takes from earlier work. "New here" means new in this design, not a claim that nobody has done it anywhere.

Plain text of this page: /text/novelty.txt

§1

NEW HERE

ruledetail
halving + claimsa burn share that halves every four weeks, with the payout share of each era paid through private claims in the next era
tier as the setone tree per holder tier per round, so the anonymity set is the tier and every claim in a tier pays the same amount
weighted amountsamount per tier from the pool by tier weight 1, 2, 3, fixed when the round opens, with tiers under 20 members left closed
roster removalnumbered removal records from the frozen program, processed in order, and a round that cannot open until every removed leaf is zeroed
unclaimed burnswhatever is not claimed in the window is sold and burned
one-way couplingthe frozen program pays into the claims inbox and never calls or reads the claims program, so the claims program cannot stop the halving or the burn

BORROWED FROM

ruledetail
Zcashcommitments to secrets, and nullifiers that mark a spend without saying which commitment it spent. introduced in the Zerocash paper (Ben-Sasson et al., 2014) that Zcash grew from
nullifiersthe per-round nullifier, Poseidon(2, secret, round), follows the same idea with an external value mixed in, as in Semaphore's external nullifier
withdraw patterna deposit-tree and withdraw-with-relayer flow with recipient and fee bound into the proof, as in Tornado Cash. developers of that project have faced criminal prosecution
Poseidona hash built for zero knowledge circuits (Grassi et al.). circomlib's parameters in the circuit, the sol_poseidon syscall onchain
Groth16Jens Groth's 2016 proof system. circom and snarkjs make the circuit and proofs. groth16-solana (Light Protocol) verifies onchain with Solana's alt_bn128 syscalls
incremental treethe frontier Merkle tree used by deposit contracts and Tornado Cash, here with leaf removal added
halvingbitcoin's block reward schedule, on a four-week clock
