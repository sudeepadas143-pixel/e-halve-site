LIMITS
------
What z/halve's private claims do not do. Each line is a limit of the design or of its current state. None of them is fixed by a config change.

rule              detail
amounts public    every claim's amount is onchain. the amount reveals the tier
tier public       a holder's tier comes from their bound wallet, which is public
anonymity set     the set is the tier's live members for that round. not all holders.
                  a tier with 20 members hides a claim among 20
relayer leaks     a relayer sees the network origin, the recipient, the tier and the time of
                  each claim. one relayer sees every claim it handles
timing leaks      a claim soon after a bind, or alone in a quiet hour, narrows the set.
                  moving funds soon after claiming does the same
operator view     the attestor and anyone reading X see which handles follow, bind a
                  wallet and bind a commitment. the roster is public by design
lost secret       a lost secret means every claim it would have made is lost. nobody can
                  recover it
weak secret       a guessable secret lets anyone link the handle to its claims
unaudited         no audit of zhalve, zhalve_claims or the circuit has been done
upgradable        zhalve_claims keeps its upgrade authority until an audit. that authority
                  could replace the program and take the inbox and the current pool
setup             the verifying key is from a local setup, not a real ceremony. whoever ran
                  it could forge proofs
tier gaming       the tier is read once, at registration. a holder can buy coin, register,
                  and sell
attestor trust    a dishonest attestor could bind its own commitment to a handle
removal timing    a removal during the claim window takes effect only when someone runs
                  deregister
unclaimed         an unclaimed share is burned. it does not roll to the holder
legal risk        unlinkable withdrawals carry legal risk. developers of a similar system
                  have faced criminal prosecution. get a lawyer before any mainnet use
not deployed      nothing has run on devnet or mainnet. the claim flow has run only in
                  LiteSVM, and on a local validator up to registration and deregister
