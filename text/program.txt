PROGRAM
-------
Two programs. Every number the other pages show comes from their accounts. Nothing is deployed, so none of these accounts exist on any cluster yet.

zhalve            Q77PgQ7norSoeP8vEur5hJwxwjd9mFrDjAKh6w2qwWg   frozen at initialize
zhalve_claims     JACSZ2GmkxtpM1J7QnqCbeXeNu9PjDGubrNQTcA38kSw   upgradeable until an audit
zhalve_venue      8aqT4YHdZWEYsuGgtxF8223MeR1cSCvrc2r7n9Wc4eY9   devnet stand-in for pump.fun. not for mainnet

zhalve contains no zero knowledge code. It never calls zhalve_claims and never reads its accounts. zhalve_claims reads zhalve's accounts and never writes them.

ZHALVE INSTRUCTIONS
-------------------
rule              detail
initialize        upgrade authority only, once. sets the attestor, coin, venue and price
                  feed. the next instruction in the same transaction must remove the
                  upgrade authority, so the program is frozen from the moment it is set up.
claim_fees        permissionless. pulls the creator share from the creator vault into the
                  clock.
tick              permissionless. checks the trigger, buys and burns, applies the era's
                  split, sells the payout share for usdc into outbound, writes the tick
                  account. takes no roster accounts and pays nobody.
advance           permissionless. once era_start is 4 weeks old, halves burn_bps, floors
                  it at 100 bps, and moves outbound to the claims inbox. skips the
                  transfer if the inbox is missing or unusable, and still halves.
upsert_roster     attestor only. appends a follower and records the roster root.
remove_roster     attestor only. removes an unfollow and writes a numbered removal record.
bind              attestor only. writes the bound wallet and the claim commitment after
                  verifying the X post. needs a non-zero post id.

ZHALVE_CLAIMS INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------
rule              detail
initialize        anyone, once. every value is read from zhalve's state.
open_round        anyone, during era r. creates round r and one empty tree per tier.
register          anyone, during era r. adds a follower's leaf to their tier's tree.
deregister        anyone. processes the next zhalve removal for a round and zeroes the
                  removed follower's leaf.
fund_era          anyone, during era r + 1, after every removal is processed. moves the
                  inbox to the pool and fixes the open tiers and amounts.
claim             anyone, during era r + 1. verifies a Groth16 proof and pays usdc.
sweep_to_burn     anyone, from era r + 2. sells unclaimed usdc, buys the coin, burns it.

ZHALVE ACCOUNTS
---------------
rule              detail
state             pda ["state"]. 332 bytes. attestor, venue, roster_len, unticked_balance,
                  tick_count, roster_root, era_start, era, burn_bps, total_burned,
                  last_funded, total_funded, removals.
clock             pda ["clock"]. holds sol. leaves only as the burn buy and the payout sale.
outbound          pda ["outbound"]. holds the current era's usdc until advance.
roster entry      pda ["roster", handle_hash]. 85 bytes. position, bound_wallet, bind_post_id.
commitment        pda ["commitment", handle_hash]. 81 bytes. the claim commitment and its post id.
removal           pda ["removal", index]. 57 bytes. one per removal, in order.
tick              pda ["tick", index]. every field listed on the ticks page.

ZHALVE_CLAIMS ACCOUNTS
----------------------
rule              detail
config            pda ["config"]. usdc mint, coin mint, pool busy flag.
authority         pda ["authority"]. owns the inbox, the pool and the burn account.
inbox             pda ["inbox"]. usdc. zhalve's advance pays into it.
pool              pda ["pool"]. usdc. one round at a time.
round             pda ["round", r]. status, removal cursor, pool amount, live members per
                  tier, open tiers, amounts, claimed total.
tree              pda ["tree", r, tier]. root, next index, frontier, live members.
registration      pda ["reg", r, handle_hash]. tier, leaf index, leaf, removed flag.
nullifier         pda ["nullifier", r, nullifier]. exists once that nullifier has claimed.

CONSTANTS
---------
rule              detail
trigger floor     0.15 SOL
burn from pool    at most 30 bps of spot (impact cap), regardless of the era's burn share
era length        4 weeks, fixed
burn share        100% / 50% / 25% / 12.5% / 6.25% ... halving each era, floor 1%
swap slippage     50 bps
tiers             1 / 2 / 3 at 0 / 0.15% / 0.75% of supply
min anonymity     20 live members per tier before it opens (config guess)
relayer fee cap   5% of the claim (config guess)
tree depth        20 (1,048,576 leaves per tier per round)
sweep dust        100 usdc base units carry into the next round
upgrade authority zhalve: none. zhalve_claims: set until an audit
