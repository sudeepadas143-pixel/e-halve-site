NOVELTY
-------
Most of z/halve's privacy is borrowed. This page separates what this repository puts together from the pieces it takes from earlier work. "New here" means new in this design, not a claim that nobody has done it anywhere.

NEW HERE
--------
rule              detail
halving + claims  a burn share that halves every four weeks, with the payout share of each
                  era paid through private claims in the next era
tier as the set   one tree per holder tier per round, so the anonymity set is the tier and
                  every claim in a tier pays the same amount
weighted amounts  amount per tier from the pool by tier weight 1, 2, 3, fixed when the round
                  opens, with tiers under 20 members left closed
roster removal    numbered removal records from the frozen program, processed in order, and
                  a round that cannot open until every removed leaf is zeroed
unclaimed burns   whatever is not claimed in the window is sold and burned
one-way coupling  the frozen program pays into the claims inbox and never calls or reads the
                  claims program, so the claims program cannot stop the halving or the burn

BORROWED FROM
-------------
rule              detail
Zcash             commitments to secrets, and nullifiers that mark a spend without saying
                  which commitment it spent. introduced in the Zerocash paper (Ben-Sasson
                  et al., 2014) that Zcash grew from
nullifiers        the per-round nullifier, Poseidon(2, secret, round), follows the same idea
                  with an external value mixed in, as in Semaphore's external nullifier
withdraw pattern  a deposit-tree and withdraw-with-relayer flow with recipient and fee bound
                  into the proof, as in Tornado Cash. developers of that project have faced
                  criminal prosecution
Poseidon          a hash built for zero knowledge circuits (Grassi et al.). circomlib's
                  parameters in the circuit, the sol_poseidon syscall onchain
Groth16           Jens Groth's 2016 proof system. circom and snarkjs make the circuit and
                  proofs. groth16-solana (Light Protocol) verifies onchain with Solana's
                  alt_bn128 syscalls
incremental tree  the frontier Merkle tree used by deposit contracts and Tornado Cash, here
                  with leaf removal added
halving           bitcoin's block reward schedule, on a four-week clock
