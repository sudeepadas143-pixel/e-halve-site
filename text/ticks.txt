TICKS
-----
Every tick the program has made, newest first. Each row is one transaction. Every field is written by the tick instruction itself, in the same transaction that did the buying, burning and selling, so a row is a receipt, not a reconstruction.

A tick fires when the clock's unticked balance is at least 0.15 SOL. reserve_before and reserve_after are the pool's SOL reserve before and after the tick's own buy. They are recorded as data. They are not the trigger.

mass_before and mass_after show what the clock held going into the tick and what was left after both arms. buy_sol and burned_tokens are the burn arm's receipt. sol_usd is the Pyth price read in the same instruction. spill_sol, spill_usdc and spill_bps are the payout arm's receipt: the SOL sold, the USDC it returned into outbound, and the era's payout share. The tick pays nobody. That USDC waits in outbound until advance moves it to zhalve_claims.

roster_len and roster_root are the roster as it stood at that moment.
