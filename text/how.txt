HOW Z/HALVE WORKS
-----------------
z/halve is a coin that halves its own burn rate on a fixed clock, the same shape as bitcoin's, compressed from four years to four weeks. Two programs run it. zhalve runs the clock, the burn and the halving. zhalve_claims pays followers. A follower claims a share of each era's payout with a zero knowledge proof that they belong to their tier, without saying which follower they are. The amounts are public. Which follower took which payment is not.

Creator fees fund the whole thing. Every trade on pump.fun pays the standard fee, and the creator's share is claimed into a program vault called the clock. zhalve has no instruction that lets anyone take from the clock. SOL only leaves through a tick, and a tick can only fire once the clock has taken in enough to be worth moving.

A tick is one instruction. It buys the coin from the pool with a slice of the clock and burns what it bought. It reads the SOL price from Pyth, sells the rest of the slice for USDC, and holds that USDC in the outbound vault. How the slice splits between burn and payout is set by the era. Nobody decides it. There is no vote, no operator switch and no discretionary schedule.

At the end of each era, advance halves the burn share and moves the era's USDC to zhalve_claims. During the next era, followers claim it. Whatever nobody claims is sold, used to buy the coin, and burned.

Nothing is deployed. Nothing is audited. See status and limits.

THE TRIGGER
-----------
The clock reads its own unticked balance, not a schedule, to decide whether a tick can fire. On every call to tick it checks the fees claimed since the last tick. If that is at least 0.15 SOL, the tick proceeds. If not, the instruction fails and nothing changes, not even the stored balance. A busy hour can fire several ticks. A quiet week fires none.

rule              detail
trigger           unticked_balance >= 0.15 SOL
who calls it      anyone. tick is permissionless
failed call       changes nothing, including the stored balance

THE ERA
-------
The era fixes the split between burn and payout. Era 0 burns everything and pays nothing. Every four weeks, anyone can call advance. It halves the burn share and gives the rest to payouts, the same curve as bitcoin's block reward on a four-week clock. At 100 bps the burn share stops halving and holds there, so the clock always burns something.

era               burn share      payout share
0 (genesis)       100%            0%
1                 50%             50%
2                 25%             75%
3                 12.5%           87.5%
4                 6.25%           93.75%
floor             1%              99%

rule              detail
era length        4 weeks, fixed
advance           anyone can call it once era_start is 4 weeks old
effect            burn_bps halves, floors at 100 bps. outbound USDC moves to the claims inbox
failed call       fails if 4 weeks haven't passed. changes nothing
claims program    cannot stop advance. if its inbox is missing or unusable, advance skips the transfer and still halves

THE BURN ARM
------------
The first thing a tick does is make the coin scarcer. It takes the era's burn share of the slice and buys the coin from the pool, capped at 30 bps of price impact. The tokens are burned in the same instruction. They are never held, sold or staked. Supply only goes down.

THE PAYOUT ARM
--------------
After the burn, the tick takes the payout share of the same slice, reads the Pyth SOL price, and sells the SOL for USDC into the outbound vault. The swap must return at least the oracle value less 50 bps or the whole tick fails. The tick writes no orders and pays nobody. The USDC waits in outbound until the era ends.

THE ROSTER AND TIERS
--------------------
The roster is the follower list of the project's X account, written onchain, one account per follower keyed by the hash of their lowercase handle. The X handle is identity only. No payment goes through X. A follower can bind a wallet and a claim commitment by posting them from the handle. The wallet sets the tier: at least 0.15% of supply is tier 2, at least 0.75% is tier 3, everyone else is tier 1. The commitment is what lets the follower claim later without revealing which handle they are.

CLAIMS
------
Each era's USDC becomes a round in zhalve_claims. During the era, anyone can register followers into their tier's tree. When the era ends, the round opens for claims. A tier opens only if it has at least 20 live members. One claim pays the same amount to everyone in a tier, and tiers are weighted 1, 2 and 3. A claim needs a Groth16 proof of membership and a nullifier, which stops the same secret claiming twice in a round. The full flow is on the private page.

rule              detail
round r           the USDC collected during era r
registration      during era r. tier read from the bound wallet at that moment
claim window      era r + 1
open tier         at least 20 live members (a config guess)
amount            pool share by tier weight, fixed when the round opens
relayer fee       public, at most 5% of the claim (a config guess)
unclaimed         sold and burned after the window, at most 30 bps of impact per call
