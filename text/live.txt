LIVE
----
Nothing is deployed, so nothing on this page has a live value yet. Once both programs are deployed, every number here comes from the same accounts the programs read. Nothing is computed off to the side.

Mass is what the clock holds, unticked. It grows only through claim_fees and shrinks only through a tick. Outbound is the USDC the payout arm has collected this era. It grows with each tick and goes to zero at advance, when it moves to the claims inbox.

Burned in total counts the coin burned by ticks and by sweeps. Funded in total counts the USDC advance has moved to claims. Claimed counts what followers have taken from the current round. The gap between a round's pool and its claimed total is what the sweep will burn.

Era decides the split. The burn share halves at each advance and floors at 100 bps. Next era is the time until advance becomes callable, not a promise that someone will call it.

mass               current SOL in the clock, unticked
outbound           USDC collected this era, waiting for advance
burned in total    coin burned by ticks, plus coin burned by sweeps
funded in total    USDC moved to zhalve_claims, all time
era                current era number
burn share         current burn_bps, halves each era, floors at 100 bps
next era           time until advance can be called
round              the round open for claims, and its status
pool               USDC in the claims pool
claimed            USDC paid out of the current round, and the number of claims
tiers              live members per tier, which tiers opened, and what one claim pays

rule              detail
mass              zhalve state.unticked_balance
outbound          zhalve outbound vault balance
burned in total   zhalve state.total_burned plus the sum of round.burned_tokens
funded in total   zhalve state.total_funded
era               zhalve state.era
burn share        zhalve state.burn_bps
next era          max(0, era_start + 4 weeks - now)
round             zhalve_claims config.pool_round and round.status
pool              zhalve_claims pool balance
claimed           round.claimed_total and round.claims
tiers             round.live_at_fund, round.open, round.amounts
