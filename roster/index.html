ROSTER

The roster is the follower list of the project's X account, written onchain — not cached, not synced on a delay you have to trust. Each follower is one account, keyed by the hash of their lowercase handle, holding a position number: the order in which they followed, oldest first. That position is what everything else on this page depends on — it's the coordinate the cursor moves along, tick after tick.

Before every tick that fires, the attestor reads the live follower list and reconciles it against what's written onchain. New followers get appended at the back. Anyone who unfollowed gets removed. If someone unfollows and follows again, they come back as a new entry at the back of the line — the roster doesn't remember old positions, only current ones. This is deliberate: following right now is the only thing that qualifies someone to be paid, not follower history.

Every tick stores a merkle root over the whole roster at that exact moment, so this page isn't the only place this information lives — anyone can independently prove a given handle was or wasn't in the roster when a specific tick fired, without trusting the frontend to have shown them the truth. Following is the only requirement to be paid. No wallet needed, no transaction, no form to fill in.

Weight is the one thing a follower can actively change about their own entry. By default everyone is weight 1. Binding a wallet — posting the address from your handle on X, then sending a memo transaction from that wallet with the post id — lets the attestor verify both sides and write the wallet onto your roster entry. From there, weight is read from the bound wallet's balance at each tick: holding at least 0.15% of supply is weight 2, at least 0.75% is weight 3. Weight only affects how a payout splits across the window when your position comes up. It never affects whether you're in the roster at all.
