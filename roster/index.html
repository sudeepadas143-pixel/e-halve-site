ROSTER

The roster is the list of accounts that follow the project on X, written onchain. Each holder on it is one account, keyed by the hash of their lowercase handle, holding a position number. The X handle is identity only. No payment goes through X.

Plain text of this page: /text/roster.txt

The attestor reads the live follow list and reconciles it against what is onchain. New entries are appended at the back. Anyone who unfollowed is removed, and the last entry moves into the gap so positions stay dense. Every removal is also written as a numbered removal record, in order. zhalve_claims reads those records to stop removed holders from claiming. Someone who unfollows and follows again comes back as a new entry at the back.

Every tick stores a root over the whole roster at that moment, so anyone can later prove a handle was or was not on the roster when a tick fired.

A holder can bind two things to their entry by posting them from the handle on X. The attestor checks the post and writes them onchain with the post id.

The first is a wallet. It sets the holder's tier: holding at least 0.15% of supply is tier 2, at least 0.75% is tier 3. Everyone else, and anyone without a wallet, is tier 1. The tier is read when the holder is registered into a round, not at every tick. The wallet is public and so is the tier.

The second is a claim commitment: Poseidon(1, secret), where the secret is 31 random bytes only the holder holds. The commitment is public and tied to the handle. It does not reveal which claim belongs to which handle. A holder without a commitment cannot claim.
