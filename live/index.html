LIVE

Every number on this page comes from the same accounts the program itself reads before a tick fires. Nothing here is computed off to the side or cached with a delay worth mentioning — it's the clock's own state, read the same way the program reads it.

Mass is what the clock is currently holding, unticked. It only grows through claim_fees and only shrinks through a tick, so between ticks this number is a direct measure of how much fee volume has landed since the last one fired. Outbound is the mirror of that on the payout side — USDC sitting in the outbound vault, written by ticks, released only by settle. If outbound is growing faster than paid, orders are backing up faster than the hand is clearing them.

Burned in total and paid in total are running sums, not snapshots — every tick adds to burned, every settled order adds to paid. Together they're the whole history of the split in two numbers: how much scarcer the coin has gotten against how many real dollars have actually reached followers.

Cursor and era describe where the mechanism is in its own cycle. Cursor is a position on the roster ring — it tells you whose turn is coming, not just that the ball has been rolling. Era is a plain count, but it's the number that decides everything about the current split: burn share is derived from it directly, halving each time advance is called, floored at 100 bps so burning never fully stops. Next era is the honest version of "when does this change" — the time remaining until advance becomes callable, not a promise about when someone will actually call it.
