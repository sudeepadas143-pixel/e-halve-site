CIRCUITS

One circuit, claim.circom, written in circom and proved with Groth16 through snarkjs. zhalve_claims verifies the proofs onchain with groth16-solana. The numbers on this page are checked against the compiled files by tests.

Plain text of this page: /text/circuits.txt

§1

WHAT A PROOF SAYS

The prover knows a secret such that Poseidon(Poseidon(1, secret), tier) is a leaf under root, that leaf is not zero, and nullifier = Poseidon(2, secret, round). It does not say which leaf. tier is 1, 2 or 3. round fits in 32 bits, fee in 64 bits, each pubkey half in 128 bits. recipient, relayer and fee are public inputs, so a proof made for one value does not verify for another.

§4

CEREMONY STATE

Local setup. Not a real ceremony. One machine made the powers of tau and one phase 2 contribution. Whoever ran it could have kept the toxic waste and could forge proofs that drain every open tier of a round. A multi-contributor ceremony is planned in CEREMONY.md. It is not done.
