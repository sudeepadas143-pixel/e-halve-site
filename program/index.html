INSTRUCTIONS

ruledetail
initializeupgrade authority only, once. sets hand, attestor, coin, venue and price feed. must be followed in the same transaction by the loader removing the upgrade authority, so the program is frozen from the moment it is configured.
claim_feespermissionless. pulls the creator share from the creator vault into the clock and adds exactly what arrived to unticked_balance. nothing to claim fails.
tickpermissionless. checks the trigger, buys and burns in the same instruction (at most 30 bps of price impact), reads Pyth SOL/USD, swaps the payout share to usdc, writes one order per window position (shrinking until each is at least $0.25), moves the cursor, writes the tick account with the roster root.
advancepermissionless. once era_start + 4 weeks has passed, halves burn_bps, floors it at 100 bps, and restarts the era clock.
settlehand only. marks an open order settled with a payment post id and releases its exact cents to the hand's usdc account.
void_orderpermissionless. voids an open order whose handle is no longer on the roster; its cents go to the next payout.
upsert_rosterattestor only. appends a follower at the back of the roster and records the new roster root.
remove_rosterattestor only. removes an unfollow, moves the last entry into the gap so positions stay dense, records the new root.
bindattestor only. writes bound_wallet after the attestor verifies the post and the memo transaction. also writes the private flag, the opt-in to the private path. bind is the only instruction that changes it. it takes no address.

ACCOUNTS

ruledetail
statepda ["state"]. 368 bytes. cursor, unticked_balance, tick_count, roster_len, roster_root, era_start, era, burn_bps, payout carry, totals.
clockpda ["clock"]. holds sol. leaves only as the burn buy and the payout swap. no withdraw path.
clock tokenspda ["clock_tokens"]. coin account owned by the clock. zero between instructions: everything bought is burned in the same instruction.
outboundpda ["outbound"]. holds usdc. released only by settle, only to the hand, only for an order that exists.
roster entrypda ["roster", handle_hash]. 86 bytes. position, bound_wallet, bind_post_id, private. tick never reads private, so it never changes an amount.
tickpda ["tick", index]. every field listed on the ticks page. written once.
orderpda ["order", tick_index, position]. handle_hash, weight, amount in cents, status, payment_post_id.
