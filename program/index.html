INSTRUCTIONS

ruledetail
tickpermissionless. checks the trigger, buys and burns, applies the era's split, swaps the payout share to usdc, writes orders, moves the cursor, writes the tick account.
advancepermissionless. once era_start >= 4 weeks old, halves burn_bps and floors it at 100 bps.
settlehand only. marks an order settled with a payment post id and releases its cents.
upsert_rosterattestor only. appends or removes roster entries and updates the stored roster root.
bindattestor only. writes bound_wallet after verifying the post and the memo transaction.
claim_feespermissionless. pulls the creator share from the pump.fun creator vault into the clock.
