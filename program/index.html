zhalve contains no zero knowledge code. It never calls zhalve_claims and never reads its accounts. zhalve_claims reads zhalve's accounts and never writes them.

§1

ZHALVE INSTRUCTIONS

ruledetail
initializeupgrade authority only, once. sets the attestor, coin, venue and price feed. the next instruction in the same transaction must remove the upgrade authority, so the program is frozen from the moment it is set up.
claim_feespermissionless. pulls the creator share from the creator vault into the clock.
tickpermissionless. checks the trigger, buys and burns, applies the era's split, sells the payout share for usdc into outbound, writes the tick account. takes no roster accounts and pays nobody.
advancepermissionless. once era_start is 4 weeks old, halves burn_bps, floors it at 100 bps, and moves outbound to the claims inbox. skips the transfer if the inbox is missing or unusable, and still halves.
upsert_rosterattestor only. appends a follower and records the roster root.
remove_rosterattestor only. removes an unfollow and writes a numbered removal record.
bindattestor only. writes the bound wallet and the claim commitment after verifying the X post. needs a non-zero post id.
§2

ZHALVE_CLAIMS INSTRUCTIONS

ruledetail
initializeanyone, once. every value is read from zhalve's state.
open_roundanyone, during era r. creates round r and one empty tree per tier.
registeranyone, during era r. adds a follower's leaf to their tier's tree.
deregisteranyone. processes the next zhalve removal for a round and zeroes the removed follower's leaf.
fund_eraanyone, during era r + 1, after every removal is processed. moves the inbox to the pool and fixes the open tiers and amounts.
claimanyone, during era r + 1. verifies a Groth16 proof and pays usdc.
sweep_to_burnanyone, from era r + 2. sells unclaimed usdc, buys the coin, burns it.
§3

ZHALVE ACCOUNTS

ruledetail
statepda ["state"]. 332 bytes. attestor, venue, roster_len, unticked_balance, tick_count, roster_root, era_start, era, burn_bps, total_burned, last_funded, total_funded, removals.
clockpda ["clock"]. holds sol. leaves only as the burn buy and the payout sale.
outboundpda ["outbound"]. holds the current era's usdc until advance.
roster entrypda ["roster", handle_hash]. 85 bytes. position, bound_wallet, bind_post_id.
commitmentpda ["commitment", handle_hash]. 81 bytes. the claim commitment and its post id.
removalpda ["removal", index]. 57 bytes. one per removal, in order.
tickpda ["tick", index]. every field listed on the ticks page.
§4

ZHALVE_CLAIMS ACCOUNTS

ruledetail
configpda ["config"]. usdc mint, coin mint, pool busy flag.
authoritypda ["authority"]. owns the inbox, the pool and the burn account.
inboxpda ["inbox"]. usdc. zhalve's advance pays into it.
poolpda ["pool"]. usdc. one round at a time.
roundpda ["round", r]. status, removal cursor, pool amount, live members per tier, open tiers, amounts, claimed total.
treepda ["tree", r, tier]. root, next index, frontier, live members.
registrationpda ["reg", r, handle_hash]. tier, leaf index, leaf, removed flag.
nullifierpda ["nullifier", r, nullifier]. exists once that nullifier has claimed.
