~/z-halve/ticks

TICKS

Every tick the program has made, newest first. Each row is one transaction — not a summary of several, not an average. The delta is how much the pool's reserve had to move since the last tick before this one was even allowed to fire.

Nothing on this page is derived after the fact. Every field here is written by the tick instruction itself, in the same transaction that did the buying, burning, and order-writing — so a tick's row is a receipt for exactly what happened, not a reconstruction of it. reserve_before and reserve_after are the pool's SOL reserve at the moment the tick checked its trigger and the moment it finished; delta is the absolute difference between them, and it's what had to clear 0.15 SOL for this tick to be allowed to run at all.

mass_before and mass_after show what the clock held going into the burn arm and what was left after the payout arm took its share — the two numbers bracket the whole instruction. buy_sol and burned_tokens are the burn arm's own receipt: how much SOL went into the pool, and how many tokens came back and got destroyed in the same instruction. sol_usd is the Pyth price read at that exact moment, which is what turns the payout arm's SOL slice into a dollar figure worth writing orders against.

spill_sol, spill_usd, and spill_bps are the payout arm's version of the same thing — how much left the clock, what it was worth, and what share of mass that represented under the current era's split. window, cursor_before, and cursor_after describe who actually got paid: how many consecutive roster positions this tick reached, and where the cursor started and ended. roster_len and roster_root are the roster exactly as it stood at that moment — the thing that lets anyone later prove a given handle was or wasn't reachable by this specific tick.
