~/z-halve/limits

LIMITS

What z/halve's private claims do not do. Each line is a limit of the design or of its current state. None of them is fixed by a config change.

Plain text of this page: /text/limits.txt

ruledetail
amounts publicevery claim's amount is onchain. the amount reveals the tier
tier publica follower's tier comes from their bound wallet, which is public
anonymity setthe set is the tier's live members for that round. not all followers. a tier with 20 members hides a claim among 20
relayer leaksa relayer sees the network origin, the recipient, the tier and the time of each claim. one relayer sees every claim it handles
timing leaksa claim soon after a bind, or alone in a quiet hour, narrows the set. moving funds soon after claiming does the same
operator viewthe attestor and anyone reading X see which handles follow, bind a wallet and bind a commitment. the roster is public by design
lost secreta lost secret means every claim it would have made is lost. nobody can recover it
weak secreta guessable secret lets anyone link the handle to its claims
unauditedno audit of zhalve, zhalve_claims or the circuit has been done
upgradablezhalve_claims keeps its upgrade authority until an audit. that authority could replace the program and take the inbox and the current pool
setupthe verifying key is from a local setup, not a real ceremony. whoever ran it could forge proofs
tier gamingthe tier is read once, at registration. a follower can buy coin, register, and sell
attestor trusta dishonest attestor could bind its own commitment to a handle
removal timinga removal during the claim window takes effect only when someone runs deregister
unclaimedan unclaimed share is burned. it does not roll to the follower
legal riskunlinkable withdrawals carry legal risk. developers of a similar system have faced criminal prosecution. get a lawyer before any mainnet use
not deployednothing has run on devnet or mainnet. the claim flow has run only in LiteSVM, and on a local validator up to registration and deregister
z/halve · not deployed · not audited
